# Community guidelines

Agent Progress welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels and ways of working. These guidelines apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces.

- Be respectful and patient. Ask questions, explain assumptions and help newcomers find their way.
- Critique ideas and code, not people's identity, ability or motives.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish someone else's personal details, credentials or private messages.
- Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, persistent personal attacks and deliberate disruption are not welcome.
- Accept constructive feedback and respect a maintainer's decision to close or redirect a discussion.

The maintainer, [@31780](https://github.com/31780), is responsible for moderating project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict participation or block repeat or severe violations. Enforcement should be proportionate and should protect the people affected.

To raise a concern, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the relevant comment or discussion. Where it is safe to discuss publicly, you can also flag the relevant conversation to the maintainer. Do not post sensitive details publicly; GitHub's **Report abuse** flow can be used for private reports of harassment or platform-policy violations.

Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting route](SECURITY.md).
